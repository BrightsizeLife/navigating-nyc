About the numbers

Walk times use Manhattan distance (|Δlat| + |Δlon|) at 1.4 m/s (~3.1 mph), which approximates walking NYC's street grid better than a straight line. They ignore actual routes, lights and hills.

Walking time to the nearest station on your selected lines, computed for every cell of a grid over the city. Dark purple is close, bright yellow is far; anything past the walk cap is left transparent.

Coverage across the mapped area

Percentages are shares of the whole bounding box, which includes open water and part of New Jersey — so treat them as a way to compare line selections against each other, not as a census of New York. The median and spread describe only the area inside your walk cap, i.e. the part of the map that is actually coloured in.

Stations further away than the walk time cap are left out of all three tables.
