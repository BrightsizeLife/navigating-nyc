method

walk times are manhattan distance (|Δlat| + |Δlon|) at 1.4 m/s — about 3.1 mph. that fits new york's grid better than a straight line, and still ignores actual routes, lights and hills. the full model is in [the readme].

privacy

no cookies, no analytics, no accounts, nothing stored. all the math runs locally. exactly three things can leave this page, all to free services: map tiles ask carto for the area you're looking at; pressing [locate] sends that one address to the photon/osm geocoder; ticking the road-network box sends sampled coordinates to osrm. that's the entire list. one heads-up: the url remembers your chosen origin so the view is shareable — share it only if you want the address shared.

walking time to the nearest station on your selected lines, for every cell of a grid over the city. dark purple is close, pale yellow is far; anything past the walk cap stays transparent. hover the map to read exact minutes.

coverage

percentages are shares of the whole bounding box — which includes open water and a slice of new jersey, so compare line selections with them rather than reading them as a census. the median and spread describe only the area inside your walk cap: the part that's actually painted.

stations further than the walk time cap are left out of all three tables.
